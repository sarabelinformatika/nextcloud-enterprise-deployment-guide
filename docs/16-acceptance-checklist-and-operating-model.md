# 16 — Acceptance Checklist and Operating Model

## Technical acceptance

- Supported release and component compatibility documented.
- DNS, TLS, proxy headers, trusted domains, and trusted proxies validated.
- MFA, privileged identities, lifecycle, and break-glass tested.
- Database, Redis locking, storage, cron, mail, apps, and clients validated.
- Security warnings reviewed and exceptions formally accepted.
- Monitoring, logging, certificate, capacity, backup-age, and cron alerts tested.
- Isolated restore completed within approved RTO/RPO.
- Upgrade and rollback procedures rehearsed.

## Handover

Record owners, support hours, escalation, maintenance schedule, access custody, architecture, dependencies, secrets location, backup policy, recovery evidence, known risks, supplier contacts, and license/support status.

Business acceptance must confirm collaboration workflows, client behavior, sharing policy, data ownership, outage communication, and recovery expectations. Re-accept after major architectural or identity changes.
