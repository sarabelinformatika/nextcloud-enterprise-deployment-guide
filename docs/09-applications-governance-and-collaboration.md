# 09 — Applications, Governance, and Collaboration

Maintain an approved-app catalog with owner, business purpose, source, license, data access, dependencies, supported Nextcloud versions, update window, rollback plan, and decommission date.

Install apps only from trusted sources and stage updates before production. Disable unused apps. Review permissions and data flows for Files, Groupware, Talk, Office, workflow automation, antivirus, full-text search, external storage, and federation.

Define sharing defaults, external-share expiry, password policy, resharing, public uploads, federated sharing, file-drop use, group folders, retention, and legal or contractual restrictions.

Document who owns content when a user leaves and how files, shares, calendars, contacts, recordings, and team spaces are transferred or retained.
