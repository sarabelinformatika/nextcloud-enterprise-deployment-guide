# 08 — Identity, MFA, LDAP, OIDC, and Lifecycle

Use named accounts, enforce MFA according to risk, separate privileged administration, and maintain a tested break-glass identity protected by strong controls.

For LDAP/AD or OIDC, document the immutable user identifier, group mapping, display-name and mail attributes, account disablement behavior, nested groups, token/session lifetime, certificate trust, IdP outage behavior, and rollback path.

Test joiner, mover, leaver, temporary access, lost-factor recovery, locked account, IdP outage, and emergency administration. Avoid shared administrator accounts.

Application passwords and device sessions require lifecycle management. Review active sessions, app passwords, connected devices, delegated access, group administrators, and dormant users regularly.
